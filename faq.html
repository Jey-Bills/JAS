FAQ

Questions, answered

Common things students and clients ask before they send a brief. If something isn't covered, message us on WhatsApp.

Before you send your brief

How do I request a quote?

Send a brief through the contact form, WhatsApp, or email. Include your level, page or slide count, deadline, and a short description of what you need. You'll receive a scoped quote before any work begins.

Are the listed prices fixed?

Most prices are ranges, not fixed fees. The listed bands are indicative. The final fee is confirmed after we review your brief. Confirmed ranges are labelled ‘Confirmed’, and estimate ranges are labelled ‘Provisional’ or ‘Target range’.

What affects the final price?

Scope, academic level, document condition, complexity, referencing requirements, deadline, and the number of revision rounds required. Urgent deadlines may attract a surcharge, confirmed in your quote.

Can I send an existing draft?

Yes — for editing and diagnostic review, an existing draft is usually required. Send it via WhatsApp or email after the initial quote is confirmed.

What if my draft needs major corrections?

If a diagnostic review shows major issues, correction work is quoted separately from the diagnosis. We don't fold major redesign into a standard editing fee.

How are urgent deadlines handled?

Normal deadlines have no surcharge. Deadlines under 48 hours may attract a surcharge. The exact amount is confirmed in your quote and subject to individual discussion.

What payment terms apply?

Small jobs are paid in full upfront. Larger jobs are 50% deposit with the balance before final delivery. Research packages use milestone payments agreed in writing before work starts.

Is my document confidential?

Yes. Your documents and personal details are treated as private and never shared, resold, or used outside the agreed scope.

Can I request a service not listed?

Yes. Send your specific request by WhatsApp or email. We'll review it and confirm the support we can offer, along with the scope and quote.

Do you guarantee grades or acceptance?

We strive to support high-quality academic work and excellent results. Final grades and acceptance depend on your institution's assessment, so specific outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

How do I pay?

Payment details are shared in your quote. Mobile money and bank transfer are the usual options. Details are confirmed before work begins.

Still have a question? Message us on WhatsApp or email and we’ll respond as soon as possible. We’re available 24/7.
