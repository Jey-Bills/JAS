Before you agree a quote

Urgency: Normal deadlines have no surcharge. Urgent deadlines (under 48 hours) may attract a surcharge, confirmed in your quote and subject to individual discussion.

Payment terms: Small jobs — full upfront. Larger jobs — 50% deposit, balance before final delivery. Research packages — milestone payments agreed in writing.
